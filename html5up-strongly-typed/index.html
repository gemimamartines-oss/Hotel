About Our Hotel

Over 50 years providing unforgettable experiences

Hilton Hotel is synonymous with excellence and world-class service. Located in the heart of the city, we offer top-notch facilities with spectacular views and personalized 24-hour attention.

Our commitment is to make every stay a memorable experience, combining elegance, comfort, and cutting-edge technology to meet the needs of our most demanding guests.

We have 300 rooms and suites, 5 restaurants, a luxury spa, a fully equipped gym, and halls for corporate and social events.
